BELM PORTAL V202 - BREAKDOWN WORKFLOW, BOSS SPARE APPROVAL & DIGITAL JOB CARDS

1. Live Breakdown Process
- Operator/Customer problem reports automatically open a Breakdown Case.
- Shows total breakdown age, current stage, responsible department, waiting time and SLA delay.
- Stage ownership: Workshop, Technician, Boss Approval, Store Keeper, Procurement, Accounts, Parts Ready, Testing, Completed.
- Delay banner shows which department is holding the machine and the recorded blocker/reason.

2. Spare Approval Process
- Workshop/Technician can request a spare from a breakdown case.
- Every new request is WAITING_BOSS_APPROVAL.
- Only the main Customer Owner/Boss can Approve or Reject.
- After approval: Store check -> Procurement -> Accounts/PI -> Parts Ready.
- Role checks are enforced in backend, not only hidden in the UI.
- Role-aware email alerts are sent as the request moves between departments.

3. Digital Job Card
- Boss/Customer Admin or Workshop Manager generates the Job Card.
- Technician is assigned from the correct customer/provider technician pool.
- Technician saves Diagnosis, Work Done, Test Result, Completion Note and completion state.
- Job Card PDF download added.
- Job Card update email alerts customer team; BELM Admin/business email is also alerted when BELM Service Provider is active.

4. Technician Performance
- Total jobs, completed jobs, completion rate, average resolution hours.
- First-time-fix rate and repeat/rework count.
- Repeat issue is auto-detected for same machine + same Job Card title completed within 30 days, with manual repeat flag also available.

5. Service Provider Visibility
- Customer maintenance team sees its own workflow in customer-managed mode.
- Customer Technician works only while customer maintenance mode is active.
- BELM Admin and BELM Technician see customer breakdown workflow only while BELM Service Provider is ON.

6. Customer Portal / Role Manager
- Machine card: Breakdown Process action.
- MORE TOOLS: Breakdown Process.
- Technician machine card: Job Card / Process.
- Role Manager permission: Breakdown Process / Job Cards (workflow).
- Workshop Manager, Store Keeper, Accountant and Procurement presets include workflow access.

Deployment
- Run backend/schema.sql after deploying V202 to create the new workflow/job-card tables and sequence.
