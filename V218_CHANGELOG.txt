BELM PORTAL V218 - TEMPORARY TECHNICIAN OVERRIDE

- BELM Technician keeps one permanent/home customer.
- BELM Super Admin or Engineer can temporarily dispatch that Technician to another customer without changing the permanent assignment.
- Engineering page now has Technician Dispatch: Technician + Customer + Job + Priority + Due Date.
- Temporary jobs appear in Technician > My Tasks with a TEMPORARY OVERRIDE badge and home-customer reference.
- Service Requests allow a confirmed Temporary Override when Super Admin/Engineer selects a Technician attached to another customer.
- Digital Job Cards allow the same override and write it to the breakdown audit timeline.
- Cross-customer Job Card access is job-scoped: the Technician may open the assigned case/report/PDF, not the customer's full portal.
- Technician /tech gets a Job Cards / Process shortcut so temporary Job Cards are reachable even when the machine is outside the permanent customer.
- Customer-managed Technicians cannot be borrowed by BELM; override is for BELM-owned Technicians only.
- No database migration is required.
