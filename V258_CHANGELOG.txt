BELM PORTAL V258 - PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS/DEFINITIONS PAGES

Added two new standalone legal pages, matching the Portal's branding:

- /legal/privacy-policy.html — what information the Portal collects
  (account, machine, financial, communications, activity logs), how
  it's used, who can see it (role-based access explained), retention,
  user rights, security, and contact info.
- /legal/terms.html — Terms of Use plus a Definitions table covering
  every core concept used across the Portal (Customer, Customer Owner/
  Assistant, BELM Service Provider vs Self-Service mode, Technician,
  Digital Job Card, Checklist Report, Breakdown Workflow, Service
  Request, Proforma Invoice, Petty Cash, Role Manager, Application/
  Registration Request), plus registration/approval rules, acceptable
  use, roles & access, service availability, record accuracy,
  termination, and contact info.

LINKED FROM
- Registration form (frontend/apply): the consent checkbox now links
  both pages, and a footer note under the submit button links them too.
- Portal Home (frontend/portal-home.html): footer links added.

NOTE
- Both pages carry a clear notice that they are a general-purpose
  draft prepared for the Portal, not legal advice, and should be
  reviewed by a qualified lawyer familiar with Tanzanian law before
  being treated as final/binding. Please review and edit the wording
  (especially retention periods and contact details) to match your
  actual practices before publishing this as official.
