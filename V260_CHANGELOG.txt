BELM PORTAL V260 - LEGAL PAGES: ENGLISH + KISWAHILI

Both legal pages (/legal/privacy-policy.html and /legal/terms.html)
are now bilingual, with a 🌐 language toggle button (matches the
dashboard's language toggle style), default English.

- Full Kiswahili translation of every section in both pages: the
  Privacy Policy (9 sections) and Terms & Definitions (13 sections,
  including the new Purpose, No-tampering, and Governing law clauses
  added in V259, and the full Definitions table).
- Toggling switches the page title, "Last updated" line, nav labels,
  the entire article body, and the legal-draft notice at the bottom -
  everything on the page, not just partial text.
- Preference is remembered per-browser (localStorage), separate from
  the dashboard's own language setting.
- New shared file: frontend/legal/legal.js handles the toggle logic
  for both pages.

- Browser/PWA cache bumped to V260.
- No database migration required.
