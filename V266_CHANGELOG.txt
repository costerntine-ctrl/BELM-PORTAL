BELM PORTAL V266 - COMPLETE ACCOUNT DELETION + CONFIRMATION EMAIL

Requested: when a customer account is permanently deleted, they
should receive a confirmation message, and no backup/history record
of their machines or other usage should remain in the system.

FOUND & FIXED - "Forget permanently" was incomplete
- belm_forget_customer_permanently() was missing cleanup for two
  tables that reference customers(id) directly with no
  ON DELETE CASCADE: `receipts` (cash payment receipts) and
  `machine_operator_shifts` (operator sign-in/sign-out history).
- Because the whole deletion runs inside one transaction, any customer
  who ever had a cash Payment/Receipt recorded, or any operator shift
  history, would make the final DELETE FROM customers fail on a
  foreign-key violation - the entire "Forget permanently" transaction
  silently rolled back, leaving the customer (and everything else)
  still fully in the database despite BELM Admin seeing a success
  message. Both tables are now cleared as part of the same permanent
  deletion, so nothing is left behind for any customer, regardless of
  their payment or operator-shift history.

ADDED - confirmation email
- Once a permanent deletion fully completes, BELM now sends a plain
  email (not an in-portal message - the account and all its records,
  including saved communications, no longer exist at that point) to
  the customer's last known email address, confirming the account and
  everything associated with it (machines, checklist/check-up reports,
  service requests, job cards, invoices, receipts, proforma invoices,
  petty cash records, operator/shift history, communications) has been
  completely removed with no backup kept and no way to restore it.
- Sent best-effort after the deletion has already committed - a
  delivery failure here never blocks or reverses the deletion itself.

UPDATED - Privacy Policy (English + Kiswahili)
- Section 6.4 (User Rights) now accurately describes this: requesting
  permanent deletion removes every associated record with no backup
  retained, and BELM confirms in writing once it's done.

- Browser/PWA cache bumped to V266.
- No database migration required (no schema change - only cleaning up
  existing tables more completely).
