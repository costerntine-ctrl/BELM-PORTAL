BELM Portal V288 - Customer Privacy Controls + Policy Update
Date: 17 August 2026

CUSTOMER PRIVACY CONTROLS
- Added "Privacy & BELM Access" to Customer Dashboard > MORE TOOLS.
- Customer Owner / Company Admin / trusted Assign Users role can control four data-sharing switches:
  1. Check-up & maintenance records
  2. Expenses, petty cash & receipt photos
  3. Store & service-parts/service-kit records
  4. Customer team/user directory
- Optional internal data is PRIVATE by default for the new privacy field.

ALWAYS AVAILABLE TO BELM
- Basic company identity/contact details.
- Registered machine identity and operational status.
- Official support/service/spare requests intentionally sent to BELM.
- Direct BELM <-> Customer communications.

SERVICE / SUPPORT EXCEPTIONS
- If BELM Service Provider is ON, maintenance/check-up and service-kit records needed to perform the active service remain accessible.
- An OPEN official support request gives temporary machine-scoped maintenance/service-kit access for that machine.
- Expense receipts and Customer team directory still require their own privacy switches.

BACKEND ENFORCEMENT
- Checklist/report/service-status access respects Customer privacy.
- Expense/receipt and petty-cash staff endpoints respect Customer privacy.
- Service-parts staff endpoints respect Customer privacy.
- BELM staff team-directory read/add/remove respects Customer privacy.
- Sensitive maintenance fields are removed from BELM customer API payloads when access is not allowed.
- Customer-managed Technicians remain internal Customer users and are not blocked from their own company data.
- Password/recovery hashes are no longer returned in the Customers Overview API payload.

BELM ADMIN UI
- Report / Check Up / Expense Receipts / Service Parts show as locked when Customer privacy blocks BELM.
- Service-due polling is skipped for privacy-locked machines.
