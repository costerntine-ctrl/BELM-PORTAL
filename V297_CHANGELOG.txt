BELM Portal V297 - Procurement-First Spare Workflow

1. Customer spare requests no longer go directly to BELM or suppliers.
2. Spare request screen now searches Customer Store by spare name or reference/part number.
3. Selected spare requirements are sent only to the Customer Procurement queue.
4. Procurement decides whether to issue from Customer Store or mark Purchase Required.
5. Procurement statuses: PENDING PROCUREMENT, PURCHASE REQUIRED, ORDERED, PARTS READY, REJECTED.
6. Store issue from Procurement atomically deducts Customer Store and creates the machine Procurement record.
7. Purchase CSV moved to the Procurement page for Procurement users; it is not a customer-to-supplier request.
8. Every Procurement request is linked into Maintenance Process. Maintenance Process shows Procurement as the current owner and mirrors spare status until PARTS READY.
9. Customer Maintenance Process Request Spare action redirects to the Procurement-first spare search screen.
10. Legacy direct-to-BELM spare endpoints are blocked for safety. Legacy Store approvals remain visible only for pre-V297 records.
11. Existing V296 Procurement records, Store ledger, receipts, reports, privacy and prior performance fixes remain compatible.
