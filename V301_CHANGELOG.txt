BELM Portal V301 - Customer Service Job Card + Billing Sync

Operational flow
- An official customer Service Request linked to a machine automatically creates/uses one Digital Job Card.
- The Job Card records Issued By / Issued At and follows assignment to the same technician as the Service Request.
- Duplicate manual Job Card generation is blocked when the customer-issued Job Card already exists.
- Job Card PDF includes issuer, technician, customer sign-off, billing state and signature lines.

Customer sign-off
- After repair/workshop completion, BELM can upload the customer-signed Job Card (PDF/JPG/PNG/WebP, max 5 MB).
- Signed copy is viewable/downloadable by authorized BELM/customer users.
- Signed upload marks the Job Card READY FOR PROCUREMENT/BILLING.

Procurement / Billing
- From the completed signed Job Card, BELM can prepare a linked Proforma or Invoice.
- Customer Procurement displays the service Job Card, signed-copy state, linked Proforma, linked Invoice, balance and payment state.
- Customer can download sent Proforma and linked Invoice PDF.
- Invoice status indicators: yellow OUTSTANDING, green PAID.
- BELM Billing uses matching outstanding/paid indicators.

Synchronization
- Proforma and Invoice records keep source_job_card_id.
- Invoice create/edit/status/payment updates the Job Card billing state.
- Receipt-based invoice payments also update the linked Job Card in the same transaction.
- Deleted linked invoices/proformas reset the Job Card billing state when appropriate.
- Customer and BELM read the same invoice/payment records, preventing duplicate debt ledgers.

Preserved
- V299 rolling-session stability.
- V300 check-up/report button colors and night-friendly styling.
- Previous Procurement, privacy, watermark, communication and performance changes.
