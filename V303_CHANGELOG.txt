BELM PORTAL V303 — ONE LOGIN FOR ALL

- Canonical login URL is now /login for BELM staff, Technicians and customer teams.
- /api/auth/unified-login remains the single credential verifier and routes users by verified role/account type.
- New customer/staff approvals and password resets now publish the same /login URL.
- Legacy /app/<customer>, /app/TECH@BELM, /app/<name>@BELM, /admin/login and /portal/login bookmarks redirect to /login.
- /tech remains a Technician workspace; unauthenticated visits redirect to /login.
- Login form accepts either account email or Customer Portal ID.
- Existing customer portal slugs remain stored for tenant identity/scoping and old-link compatibility.
- PWA start URL and home-page login button now use /login.
- Existing role permissions and backend authorization remain unchanged.
