BELM PORTAL V328 - ASSIGNED JOB CARD SELECT

1. Technician Dispatch now lists every active Customer/Service Request Job Card in RECEIVED, OPEN or ASSIGNED status.
2. Already-assigned Job Cards stay visible and selectable instead of disappearing from the dropdown.
3. Dropdown rows show status and current Technician.
4. Selecting an assigned card preselects its current Technician when available.
5. Admin/Engineer can confirm the existing assignment or explicitly reassign the Job Card to another Technician.
6. Reassignment is confirmed in the browser and recorded in breakdown/service-request history.
7. Manual JC Number / Proforma Code lookup also resolves active assigned cards.
8. Dispatch refresh reports total, waiting and assigned Job Card counts.
9. No database migration required.
