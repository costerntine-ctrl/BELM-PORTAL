BELM Operations Platform V331 - Compact Service Request Job Card Activation

- Service Request cards are compact dispatch lines with a minimum 12 mm row height.
- Line shows Company, machine/instruction summary, Assigned Technician, Job Status, and OK / Activate JC.
- Technician/status selections are staged visually and applied when OK is pressed.
- OK force-syncs/repairs the Breakdown Case + Digital Job Card, then hides the Service Request from the active inbox.
- Activated requests remain intact in HIDDEN/History; operational work continues in Engineering -> Job Cards.
- Customer Service Request description is kept as the Job Card fault/work instruction until Technician work starts.
- Details expands WhatsApp, full Job Card instructions, receipt/sync state, parts, Notes, History, and restore/hide actions.
- No schema/database migration required.
