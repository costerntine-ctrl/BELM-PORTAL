BELM Operations Platform V336 - Assigned Technician Name

- Main Job Card now shows the currently assigned Technician by name when the Job Card is ASSIGNED/IN_PROGRESS.
- The assigned Technician is selected in the inline dropdown instead of displaying a generic Select Technician placeholder.
- Reassignment remains available by choosing another Technician.
- Job Card normalization keeps camelCase and snake_case Technician fields aligned.
- Legacy Service Request assignments are used as a safe display fallback when an older Job Card row missed its copied Technician fields.
- No database migration required.
