BELM OPERATIONS PLATFORM - V338
Main Job Card Process Stage Drill-down

1. Main Job Card Process stages are now clickable:
   - Received by BELM
   - Assigned
   - In Progress
   - Spares
   - Testing
   - Completed

2. Received by BELM opens a detail panel showing:
   - Job Card number
   - who confirmed receipt/activation at BELM
   - receipt time
   - original issuer
   - direct Assign Technician / Reassign Technician dropdown for BELM Admin/Engineer

3. Assigned opens a detail panel showing:
   - assigned Technician
   - who assigned/reassigned the Technician
   - assignment time
   - current Job Card status

4. In Progress opens Technician work details:
   - Technician
   - started time
   - diagnosis
   - work done
   - latest repair/progress timeline updates

5. Spares opens the Job Card's spare requests and statuses.

6. Testing opens Workshop test/result information.

7. Completed opens return-to-service/closure information and signed Job Card state.

8. Service Request -> Job Card activation now records the BELM actor on the Breakdown timeline, so the Received stage can identify who confirmed the handoff.

9. Stage detail panel is responsive for phone/tablet and process buttons retain their existing workflow colors while gaining click/inspection feedback.

10. No database migration is required.
