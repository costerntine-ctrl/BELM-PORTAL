BELM Operations Portal V345 - Permanent Commercial Master Templates

1. The user-approved DIGITAL PROFORMA V2 and DIGITAL INVOICE V2 PDFs are now
   stored inside backend/templates as immutable master references.
2. Exact SHA-256 checksums are pinned in MASTER_TEMPLATES.sha256 and verified
   by the commercial PDF helper whenever a document is exported.
3. Proforma PDF export now uses the approved DIGITAL PROFORMA visual system:
   BELM master logo/header, website QR, Bill To, Document Details, item table,
   Important Notice, totals/validity, bank QR cards, Trading Terms and footer.
4. Invoice PDF export now uses the approved DIGITAL INVOICE visual system:
   Job Card reference, OUTSTANDING/PAID status, item table, totals, Paid,
   Balance, bank QR cards, Trading Terms and footer.
5. NMB/CRDB QR payloads are generated dynamically with the current document
   reference so a generated PI/Invoice never carries a stale PI-0498 sample
   reference.
6. Main website QR remains the approved portal.belmgeneraltech.co.tz QR.
7. Docker now installs qrencode for runtime QR generation.
8. Both BELM-admin exports and Customer Portal downloads route through the
   same master Proforma/Invoice PDF renderers.
9. The approved original master PDF files are not used as mutable data files;
   they remain preserved as the permanent design authority while live fields
   are rendered from database records.
