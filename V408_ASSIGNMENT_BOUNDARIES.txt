BELM PORTAL V408 - ASSIGNMENT & JOB CARD BOUNDARIES
====================================================

Implemented business rules
--------------------------
1. BELM Admin / Engineer can assign only BELM-managed employees and technicians.
2. Customer Admin is the only customer-side role allowed to send a machine-linked Job Card / Service Request to BELM.
3. Customer Workshop Manager / Customer Admin can assign only the customer's own managed technicians when the customer manages its own workshop.
4. Customer Technician receives and works an assigned Job Card; the technician cannot issue or assign Job Cards.
5. Customer internal Job Cards remain inside the customer's workshop and do not appear in BELM Technician Dispatch.
6. When BELM is the active service provider, customer staff cannot directly assign BELM employees. Customer Admin sends the Job Card to BELM; BELM Admin / Engineer then assigns a BELM technician.
7. BELM temporary reallocation, where available, is restricted to BELM-managed technicians; it is not a cross-ownership assignment of customer-managed staff.
