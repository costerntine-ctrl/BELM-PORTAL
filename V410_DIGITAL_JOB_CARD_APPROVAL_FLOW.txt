BELM Portal V410 - Digital Job Card approval lifecycle

- Official Customer Admin -> BELM Job Card: RECEIVED -> ASSIGNED -> IN_PROGRESS / WAITING_FOR_PARTS -> PENDING_APPROVAL -> COMPLETED.
- Customer internal Job Card: CREATED -> ASSIGNED -> IN_PROGRESS / WAITING_FOR_PARTS -> PENDING_APPROVAL -> COMPLETED.
- Customer internal Job Cards never display Received by BELM.
- Technicians can start/update only their assigned Job Cards and cannot assign or approve.
- Technician completion submits for approval; it no longer closes the Job Card.
- BELM Super Admin / Engineer approves official BELM jobs.
- Customer Admin / Workshop Manager approves customer-internal jobs.
- Reviewer can return a pending Job Card to the Technician with a required reason.
- Approval/review metadata is retained on the Job Card and PDF.
