BELM PORTAL V413 - CUSTOMER JOB CARD SOURCE + REASSIGNMENT

- Customer Workshop now uses the same Job Card Source selector as BELM Dispatch.
- Select Existing Job Card is YELLOW.
- Create Job Card is BLUE.
- Customer Admin / Workshop Manager can reassign an active internal Customer Job Card to another customer-managed Technician when the first Technician cannot continue.
- Reassignment keeps the same Job Card number and preserves diagnosis, work history, parts and timeline.
- Reassignment is recorded in the maintenance Timeline, with optional handover reason.
- Customer-side assignment is strictly limited to that customer's own customer-managed Technicians. BELM Technicians remain assignable only by BELM Admin/Engineer.
- Pending Approval, Completed and Cancelled Job Cards cannot be reassigned.
- Create mode refuses a duplicate when an active Job Card already exists; the user must use Existing Job Card.
