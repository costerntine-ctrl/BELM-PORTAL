BELM PORTAL V418 - TECHNICIAN GENERAL REPORT CENTER

Technician Dashboard > General Report now opens a full report center for the Technician's assigned customer.

Categories
1. Machine Checklist Reports
   - All checklist reports for all machines under the Technician's assigned customer.
   - Category PDF download.
   - Individual checklist PDF download.

2. Operator Reported
   - Operator problem/report history for all machines under the assigned customer.
   - OPEN / RESOLVED status and report timestamps.
   - Category PDF download.

3. Maintenance Reports
   - Current service status per machine.
   - Maintenance/service history stored on each machine.
   - Hour meter, next service, hours remaining and recorded work/requirements.
   - Category PDF download.

4. Job Card Reports
   - Digital Job Cards assigned to the logged-in Technician only.
   - Current/completed status, fault/title, repeat/rework and dates.
   - Category PDF download.
   - Individual Job Card PDF download.

Additional
- From/To period filter and All Time control.
- Mobile-responsive report center.
- Technician scope is enforced server-side.
- Based on V417, preserving Wide Machine Overview, Machine Analysis, Operator Report and all earlier Job Card workflows.
