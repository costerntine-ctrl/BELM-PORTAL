BELM PORTAL V438 - WORKSHOP ACCOUNT IN CUSTOMER PROCUREMENT

- Adds a small Workshop Account card to Customer Procurement.
- Add funds and edit total funded amount.
- Available balance auto-calculates as Workshop Account funded amount minus customer DIRECT_PURCHASE procurement spending across workshop machines.
- Customer Store issues do not deduct cash again.
- Workshop Account receipt/proof supports JPG, PNG, WebP and PDF.
- Receipt can be uploaded during Add/Edit or from the card Upload receipt button.
- Financial/workshop control roles can manage the account; other authorized Procurement users can view the live balance.
- Existing Procurement, Customer Store, receipt, Petty Cash and dashboard workflows remain separate.
