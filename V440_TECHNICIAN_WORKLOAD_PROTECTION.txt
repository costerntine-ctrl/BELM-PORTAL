BELM PORTAL V440

Technician workload protection:
- Assign Technician dropdown shows each Technician active-job count and AVAILABLE / BUSY / FULL.
- 0-2 active jobs = AVAILABLE; 3-4 = BUSY; 5+ = FULL.
- FULL technicians are disabled in the dropdown for new assignments.
- Backend also blocks adding another active Job Card at 5+ jobs.
- Technical Department - Technicians panel shows Active Jobs, In Progress, Waiting Parts, Delayed, Completed Today and workload percentage.
- Current assignment can still be confirmed without counting the same case against itself.
