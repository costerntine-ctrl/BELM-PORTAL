V448 - Customer registration now asks: TECHNICAL DEP or PORTAL-CWM

The public registration form (/apply/) for Customer / Company applications
now includes a required choice, shown after company details:

- TECHNICAL DEP - "BELM is your Service Provider - our Technicians handle
  your machine maintenance, Job Cards and Store." (default, pre-selected)
- PORTAL-CWM - "Independent - your own Admins, Technicians and Operators
  run day-to-day maintenance and your own Workshop (Store Ledger, Tool
  Issue/Return). BELM only steps in when you request support."

New customer_applications.registration_mode column
(TECHNICAL_DEP | PORTAL_CWM, default TECHNICAL_DEP) stores the choice.

On approval (PUT /applications/{id}/approve), if the applicant chose
PORTAL_CWM, the new customer is created with is_machinery_admin = 1
(Self-Service) AND workshop_module_active = 1 (Workshop Module) already
switched on - independent from day one. TECHNICAL_DEP applicants are
created exactly as before (both off, BELM as Service Provider). BELM
Admin can still change either setting afterwards from PORTAL-CWM or
Customers & Machines - this only sets the starting point.

BELM Admin's Registrations review page (/admin-applications/) now shows
"Registration type" in the application detail, and a "· PORTAL-CWM" tag
on the pending-list card summary for quick scanning, so BELM Admin sees
what the customer asked for before approving.
