BELM PORTAL V448 — STANDALONE CUSTOMER WORKSHOP SYSTEM

1. Customer Workshop is a complete customer-owned operating system.
2. Internal roles: Customer Admin/Owner, Workshop Manager, Customer Technician, Machine Operator, Store Keeper/Tools, Procurement, Accounts/Finance.
3. Operator remains strictly operation/reporting only; no spare, Store or Procurement duties.
4. Store Keeper controls stock/tool receiving and issue; Procurement controls external purchasing.
5. BELM is outside the internal role chain and is reached through an EXTERNAL SUPPORT BRIDGE.
6. Customer Admin/Owner is the formal doorway for machine Job Cards sent to BELM.
7. Workshop Module OFF locks internal Workshop UI and customer-managed Technician workshop access; BELM Support remains available.
8. Dashboard API exposes workshopModuleActive to the Customer Workshop UI.
9. BELM Customer View shows the customer's structure as customer-owned/read-only instead of replacing it with BELM roles.
10. V447 Job Card RECEIVED -> DIAGNOSIS/REPAIR -> WAITING FOR PARTS -> TESTING -> MANAGER REVIEW -> CLOSED is retained.
