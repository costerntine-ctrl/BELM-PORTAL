V449 - PORTAL-CWM rebuilt as a customer card (structure only)

Per direct instruction, PORTAL-CWM no longer shows a table. Each customer
now gets its own card, matching the same structure as the customer card
in Customers & Machines:

- CUSTOMER eyebrow, name, Active/Inactive pill
- Phone / Email / Address
- Two live switches: Non-Payment (portal-access, stops/restores the whole
  account) and Workshop Module (workshop-module) - both reuse the exact
  same backend endpoints already used in Customers & Machines, so toggling
  here or there updates the same customer record.
- A Communication History strip with a "View all" link into that
  customer's record in Customers & Machines.
- A grid of 8 numbered placeholder buttons (1-8), each a distinct color,
  with NO function, endpoint, or navigation wired to them - per instruction,
  their job will be assigned later.

Backend: GET /customers?action=cwm-overview (backend/api/customers.php)
now also returns email, phone, address and isActive alongside the
existing selfServiceEnabled/workshopModuleActive/staff counts, so the
card can render its header without a second request per customer.
