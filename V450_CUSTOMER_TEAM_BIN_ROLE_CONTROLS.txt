BELM PORTAL V450 - CUSTOMER TEAM BIN + ROLE CONTROLS

Customer Workshop Role Manager updates:
- Customer Technician now has Edit, Activate/Deactivate and Delete controls.
- Delete for Customer Technician is soft-delete and moves the account to Team Bin.
- Delete for Workshop Manager, Store Keeper, Procurement, Accounts and Operator portal users now moves the account to Team Bin instead of hard-deleting it.
- Team Bin shows deleted customer team accounts with Restore and Clear Permanently actions.
- Restore returns the account as Active.
- Clear Permanently removes reusable login/contact credentials and removes the item from the visible Bin while retaining Job Card, approval and workshop audit history.
- Deleted accounts are excluded from active Role Manager lists, analysis and user-limit counts.
- Existing machine, Job Card, approval, store, procurement, finance and BELM data are not deleted by these changes.
- V449 Management Group and V447 Job Card flow remain intact.
