BELM PORTAL V452 - CUSTOMER WORKSHOP DIGITAL CHECKLISTS

- Added Workshop > Digital Tools > Checklist Template Manager.
- Customer Admin/Owner and Workshop Manager can manage customer-owned checklist templates.
- Technician and Operator use active templates through Check Up only; no template management access.
- Create, manual items, CSV upload, Edit, Duplicate, Activate/Deactivate, Delete to Bin, Restore.
- Customer templates are isolated by customer_id and cannot be used by another customer workshop.
- Customer-specific templates are preferred during Check Up, with BELM/global templates retained as fallback.
- Existing BELM/global checklist templates remain separate.
- Existing checklist reports remain intact when a template is moved to Bin.
- V452 schema change is additive only: nullable customer_id on checklist_templates plus index.
