V473 — PORTAL-CWM LOGIN HOME

- Customer owner, Customer Admin and Workshop Manager now land on PORTAL-CWM Home when PORTAL-CWM is ON.
- Workshop Manager no longer opens Job Cards immediately after login.
- PORTAL-CWM Home uses the live customers.workshop_module_active flag.
- If PORTAL-CWM is OFF and a customer opens the home URL directly, they are returned to Customer Dashboard.
- Existing Job Card/Store/Technician backend sync is unchanged.
