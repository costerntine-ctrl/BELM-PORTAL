BELM Portal V475

1. Login page no longer auto-resumes a stored session. Browser-saved username/password may autofill, but the user must press Open My Workspace.
2. Session persistence remains unchanged after successful login.
3. BELM Admin Customer Fleet uses one vertical scroll owner only.
4. Machine Operator Message no longer traps mouse-wheel/touch scrolling; the panel grows with content.
5. Fleet dialog prevents horizontal overflow and keeps its header/close button sticky while scrolling.
