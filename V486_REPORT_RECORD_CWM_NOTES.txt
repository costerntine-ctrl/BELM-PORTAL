BELM PORTAL V486 - CWM + REPORT RECORD

- PORTAL-BELM WM Customer Overview control is slightly longer and black.
- PORTAL-CWM is a single Workshop Manager home aligned visually with PORTAL-BELM WM.
- Customer company name, address, email, contact and BELM ON/OFF service state are shown at the top.
- Customer machine entry is dynamically named <CUSTOMER NAME> MACHINES and remains customer-scoped.
- Routine Daily Report records machine information without opening a Job Card.
- Send Job Card to BELM creates an official Service Request + Digital Job Card and enters BELM TECHNICAL DEP as RECEIVED.
- The source Operator/Customer report is linked to that official Service Request so BELM workflow filters do not lose the Job Card when the customer runs its own workshop.
- Machine Report Record aggregates Daily updates, Operator/Handover records, Checklist Reports and Digital Job Cards from source tables; there is no duplicate master-history table.
- Report Record and Daily Report PDF export are included.
- Operator screen explicitly chooses Daily Report or Send Job Card to BELM.
- Self-service abnormal reports/check-ups do not silently create BELM Job Cards unless BELM support is engaged.
- Customer BELM Invoice page is read-only: View/Download only. PORTAL-CWM does not create/manage Proforma.
