BELM PORTAL V489 - DELIVERY NOTE WORKFLOW

- Added BELM Delivery Note / Delivery Receipt register for field Technicians and Workshop Management.
- Technician can prepare filters, spares, tools or other goods against a Customer, optional Machine and optional Job Card.
- Receiver details follow the supplied BELM Delivery Receipt Form: Date, Received by, On behalf of, Address, Phone, Fax, Email, condition, damage, comments, signature/name/date.
- Customer signs directly on phone/tablet signature pad. Signed record is locked from editing.
- Draft can be saved and continued later.
- Signed notes are stored in PostgreSQL with the signature and item lines.
- View and Print / Save PDF are available from the Delivery Note Register.
- Creator Technician can delete own draft. Signed record deletion requires BELM Workshop Manager/Administration authorization using the existing Delete PIN + current account password + reason; deletion is activity-logged.
- BELM customer-managed Technicians cannot issue BELM-branded Delivery Notes.
- Machine-linked SIGNED Delivery Notes appear automatically in the Customer Machine Report Record and its report export; drafts stay internal until signed.
- Delivery Notes are reachable from PORTAL-BELM WM, Technician My Tasks and Technician My Job Cards.
- Schema migration is additive only; existing business records are preserved.
