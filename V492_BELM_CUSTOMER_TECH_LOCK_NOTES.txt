BELM PORTAL V492 - BELM ON CUSTOMER TECHNICIAN LOCK

Rule:
- BELM Service ON: customer-owned Technician section is locked.
- Existing customer Technician records are preserved; nothing is deleted.
- Customer cannot add/edit/reset/delete/login/assign its own Technicians while BELM ON.
- Customer can still use machines, Daily Reports, Report Record, operators and Send Job Card to BELM according to permissions.
- BELM Workshop Manager assigns BELM Technicians to official BELM Job Cards.
- BELM Service OFF: customer Technician management becomes available again.

Protection is enforced in both UI and backend API.
