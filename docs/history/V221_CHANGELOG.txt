BELM PORTAL V221 — REPORTS & BILLING LIVE SYNC

- Reports & Comparisons now reconciles directly from Billing invoices, recorded payments and BELM company expenses.
- Outstanding is calculated as the balance AS OF the selected period end, instead of reusing the current live balance for every historical comparison.
- Historical comparisons are therefore stable even when a payment is recorded later.
- Finance cards show source record counts (invoices, payments and expenses).
- Reports page shows the last successful sync time and source.
- Sync report button replaces the ambiguous Apply report wording.
- Non-custom period changes sync immediately.
- The page auto-syncs when it regains focus, becomes visible again, and every 60 seconds while open.
- 12-month revenue trend now excludes payments belonging to soft-deleted invoices, matching the main financial summary.
- No database migration is required for V221.
