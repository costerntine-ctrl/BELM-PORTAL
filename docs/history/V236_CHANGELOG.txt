BELM PORTAL V236 - MACHINES NEEDING ACTION: CORRECT COLOR + BLINK

- Fixed: in the "Machines Needing Action" list (Action Required card),
  the status badge (GREEN/YELLOW/RED) always showed the machine's
  general condition status, even when the ONLY reason the machine was
  listed there was an overdue/soon-due service. Example from the
  report: KALMA RS45 showed a GREEN badge while its listed reason was
  "Service overdue" - a genuinely confusing/contradictory combination.
- The badge now shows whichever is worse: the machine's general
  status or its service level (RED > YELLOW > GREEN), so the color
  always matches the real urgency shown to the customer.
- Added a gentle blinking highlight (border pulse) on machines at RED
  level for extra visibility, so the most urgent items stand out at a
  glance. Respects the "reduce motion" accessibility setting (steady
  red border instead of blinking, if the browser/OS requests it).
- Browser/PWA cache bumped to V236.
- No database migration required.
