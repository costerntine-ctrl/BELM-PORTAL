BELM PORTAL V305 - TECHNICIAN JOB CARD WORKSPACE

1. Technician Dispatch is Job-Card-first
   - Select Received Job Card
   - Create Job Card
   - Assigns the selected/created Job Card directly to the Technician
   - Temporary Override remains supported without changing permanent customer assignment
   - Priority and Due Date are stored on the Digital Job Card

2. Dedicated Technician My Job Cards workspace
   - Active Job Cards
   - My Reports
   - Simplified work process only (Assigned / Diagnosis / Repair / Testing / Completed)
   - Waiting spare/approval state shown without full department-management workflow
   - Update Job Report from the Job Card
   - Request Spare from the Job Card
   - Download Job Card / completed Report PDF for reference

3. Full Maintenance Process removed from Technician UI
   - Old Technician workflow links redirect to My Job Cards
   - Customer/Workshop/BELM management workflow remains unchanged for authorized management users

4. Received Service Job Card sync
   - Assigning a received Job Card advances the case to Technician Diagnosis
   - Official Service Request assignment is synchronized to the same Technician

5. Deploy safety
   - Health schema version: 305-technician-job-cards
   - Health checks priority/due_date Job Card columns
