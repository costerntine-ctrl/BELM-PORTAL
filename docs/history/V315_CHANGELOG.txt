BELM PORTAL V315 - JOB CARD RECEIPT CONFIRMATION

FIXED
- A Customer Service Request already created the one official Job Card with status RECEIVED, but the UI did not clearly confirm that the Job Card had arrived at BELM.
- The Service Request POST response now returns the linked Job Card number, status and received time.
- The Customer Service Request success banner now says JOB CARD RECEIVED BY BELM and shows the Job Card number.
- Engineering > Service Requests now shows a green JOB CARD RECEIVED BY BELM banner on each machine-linked request, including Job Card number, received time and current status.
- Engineering > Job Cards / Breakdown Workflow now labels the first process step Received by BELM and shows an explicit BELM Receipt confirmation on the Job Card.
- Customer machine Service Job Billing now has a BELM receipt column so the customer can verify that the Job Card reached BELM even after it progresses to ASSIGNED / IN PROGRESS / COMPLETED.
- If a machine-linked Service Request somehow has no linked Job Card, Engineering shows JOB CARD RECEIPT NOT CONFIRMED instead of silently hiding the problem.

NO DATABASE MIGRATION REQUIRED
- This is a response/UI visibility fix using the existing digital_job_cards RECEIVED lifecycle.
- Service Worker cache and health schema version bumped to V315 to prevent stale V314 UI.
