BELM PORTAL V341 - REQUEST -> PROFORMA -> INVOICE DIRECT SYNC

- Invoice is generated directly from Proforma; Billing no longer asks staff to choose Spare Parts again on a new Invoice.
- Generated Invoice copies customer, machine, Job Card, part numbers, descriptions, quantities, units, unit prices, discount amount, VAT amount/rate, terms and grand total.
- Generated Invoice is linked to its Proforma and commercial lines are locked to prevent Proforma/Invoice drift.
- Proforma is locked while its generated Invoice is active; cancel the Invoice first if commercial terms must change.
- Pending Spare Requests now appear in Billing > Proforma and are grouped by customer + machine into one multi-line Proforma.
- Spare Parts Manager also supports selecting multiple requests for one Proforma.
- Job Card Proforma preparation automatically includes non-rejected spare requests recorded against that Job Card.
- Direct Prepare Invoice was removed from Job Card workflow; signed Job Cards feed Proforma first, then Invoice.
- Duplicate active Invoice generation is prevented by source Proforma and source Job Card.
- Additive schema columns/table are auto-ensured by Billing/Proforma and included in schema.sql.
