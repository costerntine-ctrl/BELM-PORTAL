Fix: self-heal the missing Job Card assignment from the linked Service Request for the logged-in Technician; never replace a non-null different Technician assignment.
Authorization: the same narrow recovery is used by assigned Job Card actions so a recovered card is usable, not merely visible.
UI: logged-in Technician identity is shown in the My Job Cards hero and on each Job Card heading.
Cache: technician assets and service worker moved to V342.
Schema: unchanged; no migration.
