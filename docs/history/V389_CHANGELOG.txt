BELM PORTAL V389 - CUSTOMER FINANCE REPORT

Scope: Customer portal only.

Changes:
- Added Finance Report as the ninth category in Customer -> General Report.
- Finance Report shows invoice totals, payments received, outstanding balance, published proformas, proforma value, machine expenses, fuel cost, and Petty Cash figures.
- Added recent Invoice and Proforma tables with PDF access using existing customer-secured endpoints.
- BELM Admin and BELM Technician flows are unchanged.
