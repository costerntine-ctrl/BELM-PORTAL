BELM PORTAL V392 — TECHNICIAN DAILY CHECK STAMP

- Technician machine card shows the completed daily Check Up immediately after save.
- Stamp format: Technician checked DD/MM/YYYY - HH.MM · Checklist No. CHK-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXXXX.
- Checklist number is generated automatically and deterministically from the persisted checklist report.
- Time is Tanzania time and intentionally contains no seconds.
- Daily checked stamp auto-hides at 00.00 Tanzania time.
- At the new Tanzania day, Check Up becomes due again even if the last condition remains GREEN.
- Machine card updates immediately after save and then refreshes from the server sync source of truth.
- No BELM Admin or Customer machine-management behavior is changed.
