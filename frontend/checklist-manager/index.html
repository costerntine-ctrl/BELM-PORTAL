New template

History protection: do not delete historical reports when changing a template. Every submitted checklist remains a separate report record with its saved answers, date/time and technician details.

Checklist items

All required items must be completed before a technician can submit a report. Machine-specific Universal items may be optional.

CSV columns: Item Label, Input Type, Default Safety, Required, Dropdown Values. Input Type is one of TEXT, NUMBER, YES_NO, DROPDOWN, DATE, PHOTO. Required is YES or NO. For DROPDOWN, list Dropdown Values as Value:COLOR pairs separated by | (e.g. Normal:GREEN|Attention:YELLOW|Critical:RED|N/A:NONE). Uploaded items are added to the list for review; nothing is saved until Save template is clicked.
