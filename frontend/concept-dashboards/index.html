BELM Portal — Concept Dashboards

Muhtasari wa dashibodi 8 za concept zilizotengenezwa kwa mujibu wa mtiririko wa kazi za BELM (usajili wa wateja, mashine, Job Cards, inspection/repair, procurement, store, finance na operator). Hizi ni maonyesho ya muonekano (frontend tu, data tuli) kwa ajili ya idhini kabla ya kuunganishwa na backend halisi ya PHP/PostgreSQL.

CONCEPT — bado hazijaunganishwa na backend
01 Super Admin Admin Home Dashboard Muhtasari mkuu: active customers, machines, job cards, pending approvals, workshop activity na recent activity. Fungua dashibodi → 02 Technician Technician Dashboard Kazi alizopangiwa fundi, communication history, na Active Job Cards zenye next-action per status. Fungua dashibodi → 03 Procurement Procurement Dashboard Spare Purchase Priority, Pending Proforma kwa wasambazaji, na Delivery Tracking stepper. Fungua dashibodi → 04 Super Admin Customer Registration & Management Usajili wa kampuni mpya (TIN/VRN/mkataba), portal access na jedwali la wateja walioandikishwa. Fungua dashibodi → 05 Workshop Manager Inspection, Diagnosis & Repair Service Workflow (Opened→Completed), Active Job Cards na Latest Inspection Findings. Fungua dashibodi → 06 Store Keeper Store Keeper Dashboard Inventory Alerts, Pending Spare Requests na Recent Stock Movements + Quick Actions. Fungua dashibodi → 07 Machine Operator Machine Operator Dashboard Mashine aliyopangiwa, Daily Safety Checklist, Machine Alerts na Operation & Fuel Log. Fungua dashibodi → 08 Machine Operator Daily Machine Checklist Ukaguzi wa kina wa mfumo 7 (Engine, Transmission, Brakes, Hydraulic, Electrical, Tyres, Working Params) wenye Normal/Attention/Unsafe/N-A. Fungua dashibodi →
Kila folda ina index.html + assets/css + assets/js zake — huru kutoka kwa nyingine, hivyo unaweza kuzitazama moja moja bila kuathiri BELM Portal ya sasa. Ukishaidhinisha muonekano, tunaweza kuanza kuunganisha kila dashibodi na routes/PHP endpoints halisi za portal.
