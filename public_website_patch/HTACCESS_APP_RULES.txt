Add these rules inside the existing public_html/.htaccess RewriteEngine On section, BEFORE the WordPress/website catch-all rules:

# V303 canonical login used by BELM staff, Technicians and customer teams
RewriteRule ^login/?$ /belm_app.php [L,QSA]

# Legacy V289 friendly links remain compatible and converge on the same login
RewriteRule ^app/?$ /login [R=302,L]
RewriteRule ^app/([A-Za-z0-9@-]+)/?$ /login [R=302,L]

Canonical public login:
https://belmgeneraltech.co.tz/login

Old links such as /app/ecls-icd, /app/TECH@BELM and /app/john-doe@BELM continue to work by redirecting to /login.
The wrapper opens https://portal.belmgeneraltech.co.tz/login full-screen.
