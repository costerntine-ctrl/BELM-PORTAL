Add these rules inside the existing public_html/.htaccess RewriteEngine On section, BEFORE the WordPress/website catch-all rules:

RewriteRule ^app/?$ /belm_app.php [L,QSA]
RewriteRule ^app/([A-Za-z0-9-]+)/?$ /belm_app.php?customer=$1 [L,QSA]

This makes customer links stay readable on the main website, for example:
https://belmgeneraltech.co.tz/app/ecls-icd
https://belmgeneraltech.co.tz/app/belm

The wrapper opens the secure portal full-screen and can be installed to the phone home screen as an app shortcut.
